Website Topic

Quest World is a student website about escape rooms, puzzles and themed adventures.

It helps users browse quests by genre, difficulty, rating and price.

📊

About the Dataset

The custom dataset stores escape room quest objects.

Each object includes name, genre, difficulty, players, price, rating and image.

⚙️

JavaScript Features

The Quests page uses Handlebars to render cards dynamically.

Users can add, delete, search, filter and sort quests, with data saved in localStorage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an escape room?

It is a game where players solve puzzles in a themed room before time runs out.

How many people can play a quest?

Most quests are made for small teams, usually from 2 to 6 players.

Are quests scary?

Not all quests are scary. Some are mystery or adventure based and focus more on puzzles.

How long does a quest usually last?

A standard quest often lasts around 60 minutes, but some can be shorter or longer.

Why are quests popular?

People enjoy them because they mix teamwork, logic, story and challenge in one activity.
